# EventPhoto.ca competitor review and improvements — 8 November 2026

## Research method

Reviewed five sites surfaced prominently in web searches for “corporate event photographer Toronto conference photography” and “corporate event photography Canada Montreal Vancouver”. This is a search-result sample, not a verified Google rank report; rankings vary by query, location and date. Competitor statements are their own marketing claims, not independently verified delivery results.

| Site | Relevant strength | EventPhoto application |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Conference Media](https://conferencemedia.ca/conference-photography-toronto/) | Links coverage to communications, sponsors and future events; explains delivery options. | Original outcome-led copy, relevant add-on links and a deliverables checklist. |
| [Ymaphoto](https://www.ymaphoto.com/events) | Extensive event imagery, clear coverage formats and planner-oriented information. | A deeper coverage gallery with filters and an accessible enlargement viewer; clearer planning choices. |
| [Uptown Media](https://www.uptownmedia.ca/) | Corporate-first positioning, real project context and prominent client proof. | More explicit corporate positioning and a clearer path into coverage and inquiries. Actual client proof remains dependent on owner-approved material. |
| [Sheldon Isaac Images](https://www.sheldonisaac.com/corporate-event-photography-toronto/) | Coverage tiers, clear inclusions and relevant headshot/video extensions. | Three coverage starting points, explained quote scope and related add-on services. |
| [CEP Studio](https://cepstudio.com/) | EN/FR navigation, practical booking questions and explicit delivery expectations. | Preserved bilingual quote details and attachments, localized planning guidance and specific page descriptions. |

No competitor photographs, testimonials, logos, package rates, guarantees or copy were reused. Existing illustrative imagery is presented as coverage examples rather than completed EventPhoto assignments. No invented client history, reviews, prices, insurance claims or response-time promises were added.

## Implemented

- Single-page customer inquiry: essential contact/location details first; optional planning fields collapsed; existing Formspree endpoint and payload preserved.
- Tab-specific draft persistence across language switches and navigation, including attachments. Drafts expire after two hours of inactivity; successful submission clears them. Attachment storage failures show a visitor-facing message. Privacy wording explains local draft storage.
- Language links retain query parameters; previously edited fields are not overwritten by the same incoming preset after a language switch.
- Six expandable service groups cover all 39 existing services without removing established URLs.
- English and French coverage pages with three planning formats, a quote checklist and links for portraits, video, sponsor photography and urgent selects.
- Homepage coverage comparison, more explicit corporate copy, a second planning CTA and outcome-oriented content.
- Gallery expanded from 8 to 24 distinct image files with category filters, counts, contextual captions and a keyboard-accessible dialog.
- Page-specific metadata for main conversion pages; coverage routes added to bilingual sitemap; image sitemap updated to current imagery.
- French city names corrected in directories; duplicate city introductions replaced with planning questions.

## Validation

- Seven passing payload/draft tests (including consent, attachments, corrupt/expired drafts and context retention).
- All 256 routes rendered: 128 EN / 128 FR; 7,518 internal links and 850 image references checked; one H1, JSON-LD, canonical/hreflang and sitemap checks passed.
- All 39 services remain included exactly once in the new groups; 24 gallery file paths are distinct.
- No live inquiry submitted during testing; email delivery was previously confirmed by the owner.
- Managed preview reported running, but browser access failed. Checkout execution profile was repaired; browser QA continues against the published site where available.

## Owner inputs for a future proof-of-work upgrade

Approved real event galleries, client testimonials and logo permissions; actual standard delivery terms; commercially approved package rates if prices should be published.
